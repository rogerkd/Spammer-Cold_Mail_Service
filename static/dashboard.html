Delivery monitoring & sending information

Automatic delivery monitoring starts with the dashboard.
Automatic checks inspect Gmail All Mail (Inbox and archived mail) plus Spam for delivery notices matching recent tracked sends. Confirmed address-not-found bounces are blocked and removed from Contacts; temporary or unclear delivery issues are flagged for review. A missing notice does not prove delivery.
Email sending is irreversible. Review the recipient and personalized message before confirming a send.
